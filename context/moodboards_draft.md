# Style & Direction: 3 Moodboards for Fibromyalgia Tracker

To establish the visual personality of the app while honoring the accessibility, low-stress, and fast-logging requirements, here are 3 distinct design directions. Each moodboard defines its physical reference scene, color palette, typography, texture/surface treatment, and emotional impact.

---

## Moodboard 1: "Botanical Sanctuary" (Warm Calming Light Mode)

> **Reference Scene:** Crushed sage, warm limestone, unbleached linen, and soft herbal tea.

- **Emotional Impact:** Restorative, grounding, non-clinical. Feels like a supportive wellness companion rather than an illness monitor. Reduces visual stress and sympathetic nervous system activation.
- **Palette:**
  - **Base Canvas:** Linen Warm White (`#FBF9F5`)
  - **Surface / Card Background:** Soft Oat (`#F3EFEA` / `#FFFFFF` subtle card borders)
  - **Primary Calming Accent:** Deep Moss / Forest Sage (`#2D5A46`)
  - **Secondary Supporting Tone:** Muted Terracotta / Clay (`#C47151`) — for mild warnings without panic
  - **High Pain / Flare Indicator:** Soft Plum / Rose Damask (`#8C3B4E`) — distinct from aggressive emergency red
  - **Text:** Deep Espresso / Slate Bark (`#1E2220`) for high contrast readability
- **Typography:**
  - Display / Numbers: Editorial, humanistic sans-serif or clean serif (e.g., _Fraunces_ or _Plus Jakarta Sans_)
  - Body & Labels: _Plus Jakarta Sans_ or _Inter_ (medium-to-bold weights for easy reading during brain fog)
- **UI Treatment:**
  - Gentle rounded corners (16px–24px)
  - Subtle 1px warm borders (`#E5DFD7`)
  - Soft, pill-shaped tactile buttons with generous touch targets (52px)
  - Text-forward badges and soothing progress indicators

---

## Moodboard 2: "Deep Solace" (Muted Low-Strain Dark Mode)

> **Reference Scene:** Midnight overcast, wet slate, river stone, and soft glowing amber lantern.

- **Emotional Impact:** Built specifically for light sensitivity (photophobia) and evening/bedside logging during flares. Extremely gentle on fatigued eyes with zero harsh glare or saturated neon.
- **Palette:**
  - **Base Canvas:** Deep Charcoal Slate (`#111418`) — softer than pure OLED black to reduce contrast halos
  - **Surface / Card Background:** Weathered Stone (`#1A2027`)
  - **Border / Divider:** Subtle Slate Line (`#27313D`)
  - **Primary Calming Accent:** Muted Seafoam / Celadon (`#5EEAD4`) — gentle glow that stands out softly
  - **Pacing / Energy Metric:** Warm Amber / Candlelight (`#F59E0B`)
  - **Pain / Flare Accent:** Soft Burnt Coral (`#FB7185`)
  - **Text Primary:** Off-white Mist (`#E2E8F0`)
  - **Text Secondary:** Muted Stone Grey (`#94A3B8`)
- **Typography:**
  - Modern, crisp grotesque sans-serif (_Inter_ or _DM Sans_)
  - High-weight numbers (700+) with tabular spacing for instantaneous numeric scanning
- **UI Treatment:**
  - Deep layered surfaces with dark borders rather than harsh drop shadows
  - Large glowing pill states for active selections
  - Generous negative space between rows to prevent accidental mis-taps

---

## Moodboard 3: "Editorial Clarity" (Minimalist Swiss Clinic & Paper)

> **Reference Scene:** Architectural vellum, precise ink, graphite markings, and cobalt precision.

- **Emotional Impact:** Objective, organized, and scientifically clear. Treats the user like an empowered researcher of their own body. Focuses on pure data clarity, high scannability, and straightforward efficiency.
- **Palette:**
  - **Base Canvas:** Architectural Crisp White (`#FFFFFF`) or Crisp Fog (`#F8FAFC`)
  - **Surface / Card Background:** Cool Chalk (`#F1F5F9`)
  - **Primary Accent:** Precision Cobalt (`#2563EB`) — authoritative, clean, and rational
  - **Pain Severity Scale:** Monochromatic grayscale gradient accented with purposeful cobalt / ochre
  - **Text Primary:** Pure Ink (`#0F172A`)
  - **Text Secondary:** Steel Grey (`#64748B`)
- **Typography:**
  - Strictly Swiss-inspired modernist sans-serif (_Inter_ / _Helvetica Neue_ style)
  - Dramatic scale contrast: heavy, punchy display metrics (32px–40px) paired with small, tracked-out micro-caps (`UPPERCASE`, 11px, letter-spacing: 0.06em)
- **UI Treatment:**
  - Clean modular grid containers with crisp 1px borders (`#E2E8F0`)
  - Flat buttons with strong solid color fills and sharp contrast
  - Minimalist data tables and line-art correlation charts
